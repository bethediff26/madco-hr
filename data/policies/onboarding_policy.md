**Department:** Human Resources — People Operations

## 1. Purpose

The Mad Company New Hire Onboarding Policy establishes the standardized framework for integrating new employees into the organization. This policy defines the orientation process, IT provisioning timelines, mandatory compliance training requirements, review schedules, and peer mentoring structures designed to accelerate new hire productivity, foster cultural alignment, and ensure regulatory compliance from day one of employment. Consistent application of onboarding procedures reduces early turnover risk, accelerates time-to-competency, and supports equitable treatment across all departments, locations, and employment classifications.

---

## 2. Scope and Applicability

This policy applies to all new full-time, part-time, and contractor hires joining Mad Company, regardless of role level or geographic location. It covers the onboarding experience from offer acceptance through the first ninety (90) days of employment. External consultants engaged for discrete projects outside the scope of regular employment may be exempt from specific training modules at the discretion of the hiring manager and HR Business Partner, provided that all applicable legal and security requirements are satisfied.

## 3. First-Week Orientation Program

### 3.1 Day One — Welcome and Administrative Completion

The first day of employment is dedicated to foundational orientation activities:

| Time | Activity | Facilitator |
|---|---|---|
| 9:00 AM – 10:30 AM | HR Welcome Session: Company history, mission, values, organizational structure overview; introduction to benefits enrollment; completion of I-9 and tax withholding forms; signature of employment agreements (offer letter, confidentiality agreement, IP assignment). | HR Business Partner / Recruiting Team |
| 10:30 AM – 11:30 AM | IT Equipment Pickup and System Setup: Collection of company laptop, access badge, peripheral devices; configuration assistance from IT Helpdesk; initial login to Microsoft 365, Workday, and internal communication platforms. | IT Helpdesk / Systems Administrator |
| 11:30 AM – 12:00 PM | Manager One-on-One Welcome Meeting: Role expectations review, team introductions, first-week priorities discussion. | Direct Manager |
| 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM | Virtual or in-person team lunch (remote teams receive a $50 meal stipend via the expense system). | Team Lead |
| 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM | Company Culture Workshop: Values-based training session covering collaboration norms, decision-making frameworks, diversity and inclusion principles, and communication protocols. | HR Learning & Development |
| 3:00 PM – 4:30 PM | Safety Training: Workplace safety orientation (physical office evacuation routes, emergency procedures, EAP resources; for remote employees, home office ergonomic safety checklist). | Facilities Management / IT Security |
| 4:30 PM – 5:30 PM | Buddy Introductions and First Week Self-Study Assignment Review. | Assigned Buddy |

### 3.2 Days Two Through Five — Departmental Integration

During the remainder of the first week, new hires engage in department-specific orientation:
- **Departmental Orientation (Day 2–3):** Role-specific process training, system access for departmental tools, introduction to key stakeholders and cross-functional partners.
- **Shadowing Assignments (Day 3–4):** Paired shadowing with an experienced team member to observe live workflows, client interactions, or development cycles as applicable to the role.
- **First Task Assignment (Day 5):** A low-risk, high-visibility task designed to build confidence and integrate new hires into daily operations. The task should be completable within one business day and provide opportunities for feedback.

## 4. IT Equipment Provisioning Timelines

### 4.1 Standard Provisioning Schedule

IT equipment provisioning follows a strict timeline to ensure new hires can begin productive work from their first day:

| Item | Delivery / Availability Target |
|---|---|
| Company laptop (configured with approved software stack) | Shipped within **five (5) business days** of signed offer acceptance; for domestic on-site starts, delivered with tracking number provided to the new hire and manager. For remote employees, shipped via overnight express delivery to the address on file. |
| Employee access badge (physical office workers) | Issued at HR Welcome Session on Day 1. Temporary visitor badges are issued upon request for up to five business days while permanent badges are being engraved. |
| System accounts provisioned | All role-access-matrix accounts created and activated **same day as start date**, including email, VPN, HRIS, CRM/ERP (as applicable), project management tools, and communication platforms. Access provisioning must be confirmed to the new hire by 12:00 PM on Day 1. |
| Departmental software licenses | Provisioned within **two (2) business days** of start date; any specialized software not available in the standard build requires a separate IT ticket with an expected delivery window communicated to the manager. |

### 4.2 Access Matrix Compliance

IT provisions are governed by a role-based access matrix maintained by IT Security and updated quarterly. New hires receive only the minimum level of system access required for their specific job function, in alignment with the principle of least privilege. Requests for elevated access beyond the standard matrix must be approved by the department head and IT Security concurrently.

## 5. Mandatory Compliance Modules

### 5.1 Completion Deadlines and Requirements

All new hires must complete the following compliance training modules within specified timeframes:

| Module | Deadline | Format | Certification Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Workplace Harassment Prevention | Within first **seven (7) calendar days** of start date | Online module (2 hours); in-person session offered quarterly for on-site hires | Pass quiz with minimum 80% score; certificate stored in Workday learning records |
| Code of Conduct Acknowledgment | **Day one** (signed electronically during HR Welcome Session) | Electronic acknowledgment; annual re-certification required | Digital signature; stored in employee personnel file |
| Data Security Certification | Within first **thirty (30) calendar days** of start date | Online module (90 minutes) per Data Security Policy, Section 9 | Pass assessment with minimum 75% score; certificate filed in IT Security records |
| Anti-Fraud and Compliance Overview | Within first **sixty (60) calendar days** | Webinar or self-paced eLearning (1.5 hours) | Completion confirmation logged by HR |

### 5.2 Extension Requests

New hires who are unable to meet compliance module deadlines due to business-critical onboarding demands, medical leave, or travel constraints may request a deadline extension through their manager and HR Business Partner. Extensions must not exceed fourteen (14) calendar days beyond the original deadline and require written justification. Extensions granted for data security certification require temporary limited-access IT provisions with enhanced monitoring until compliance is achieved.

## 6. 30-60-90 Day Review Schedule

### 6.1 Purpose and Structure

Structured performance reviews at 30, 60, and 90 days ensure that new hires are tracking toward role proficiency and identify opportunities for coaching, additional training, or early intervention if performance gaps exist. Each review meeting includes the new hire, direct manager, and (for the 90-day review) the assigned buddy as an observer.

### 6.2 30-Day Milestone Review

**Focus:** Orientation completion confirmation, cultural integration assessment, initial task performance evaluation.

| Checkpoint Criteria | Evidence Expected |
|---|---|
| All compliance modules completed or on track for timely completion | Workday training dashboard screenshot |
| System access adequate for role responsibilities | Manager confirmation of no outstanding IT tickets |
| Understanding of team goals and individual responsibilities | Documented goal alignment meeting notes |
| Buddy relationship active and responsive | Self-reported satisfaction survey score of 3+ (out of 5) from new hire |

### 6.3 60-Day Milestone Review

**Focus:** Competency development, workflow proficiency, peer collaboration assessment.

| Checkpoint Criteria | Evidence Expected |
|---|---|
| Completion of role-specific training modules | Training completion records |
| First deliverables submitted to standards | Manager quality assessment and feedback documentation |
| Participation in team meetings and cross-functional communication | Attendance records; peer feedback on communication effectiveness |
| Identification of skill gaps requiring additional support | Individual development plan (IDP) draft, if applicable |

### 6.4 90-Day Milestone Review (Probationary Period Conclusion)

**Focus:** Full competency evaluation, probationary period decision, long-term goal setting.

| Checkpoint Criteria | Evidence Expected |
|---|---|
| Probationary period goals fully assessed against original job description | Performance scorecard signed by manager and employee |
| Demonstration of role-critical skills to acceptable standard | Work product evaluation by manager and at least one peer reviewer |
| Positive cultural alignment and team integration assessment | Buddy debrief report; 360-degree peer feedback summary (collected for individual contributors) or manager-direct feedback (for managerial roles) |
| Formal go/no-go decision on continued employment | Written determination communicated to employee within five (5) business days of the 90-day review date |

### 6.5 Probationary Period Extension or Early Confirmation

If a new hire's performance at the 60-day checkpoint indicates significant gaps but the manager believes remediation is feasible, a written probationary extension of up to thirty (30) additional days may be granted with a documented improvement plan. The final 90-or-120-day review determines confirmed employment status. Conversely, an outstanding new hire may receive early confirmation of permanent status at the manager's recommendation with HR approval.

## 7. Buddy Assignment Program

### 7.1 Assignment Process

Every new hire is paired with a peer mentor (the "Buddy") from their own team or department. Buddies are selected based on:
- Minimum tenure of six (6) months with the company.
- Positive performance history and demonstrated cultural alignment.
- Completion of an internal Buddy Mentor orientation workshop.
- Voluntary participation confirmed during annual peer mentor recruitment.

Buddy assignments are made by HR at least three (3) business days before the new hire's start date. The buddy is notified via Workday assignment notification and receives an introductory briefing on their responsibilities.

### 7.2 Buddy Responsibilities

During the ninety-day buddy relationship, the assigned buddy is expected to:
- Initiate a one-on-one introduction meeting within the first two business days.
- Provide informal guidance on daily operations, team norms, and unwritten cultural practices.
- Review the new hire's work products for quality and alignment at least once weekly during the first thirty days.
- Serve as a point of contact for questions the new hire may feel uncomfortable raising with their manager.
- Complete a 90-day debrief meeting with the new hire and manager to share observations about integration progress and any concerns.

### 7.3 Buddy Relationship Duration

The buddy relationship formally concludes at the end of the ninety-day period but is not prohibited from continuing voluntarily beyond that timeframe. Buddies receive recognition in their annual performance review and a nominal token award ($50 gift card) upon successful completion of each new hire's probationary period.

---

## 8. Post-90-Day Integration Check-Ins

Following the conclusion of the formal probationary period, new hires participate in semi-annual integration check-ins at six (6) months and twelve (12) months post-hire date. These check-ins focus on longer-term career development, benefits utilization awareness, compensation benchmark alignment, and identification of learning and growth opportunities. Check-in outcomes are documented in the employee's Workday profile and inform annual performance review preparation.
