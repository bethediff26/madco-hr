# Remote Work Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Revised:** September 1, 2026  
**Version:** 3.5  
**Approved By:** VP of Human Resources  
**Department:** Human Resources — Work Arrangements  

---

## 1. Purpose

The Mad Company Remote Work Policy establishes the framework for flexible work arrangements, including fully remote and hybrid options, to support employee well-being, productivity, and organizational goals. This policy ensures consistency in remote work practices while maintaining company culture, collaboration, and operational effectiveness.

All employees are eligible for remote work arrangements subject to their role's requirements, manager approval, and compliance with this policy.

---

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Role Requirements
Remote work eligibility is determined by job function and operational needs:
- **Eligible Roles**: Positions that can be performed effectively from remote locations, including but not limited to software engineers, designers, content writers, analysts, and administrative staff.
- **Non-Eligible Roles**: Positions requiring physical presence (e.g., lab technicians, security personnel, manufacturing roles) or client-facing responsibilities that necessitate in-office presence.

### 2.2 Employee Tenure
Employees must have completed at least six months of continuous employment to be eligible for remote work arrangements.

### 2.3 Performance Standards
Employees must demonstrate consistent performance and productivity to qualify for remote work arrangements. Remote employees are expected to maintain or exceed the performance standards of their in-office counterparts.

---

## 3. Types of Remote Work Arrangements

### 3.1 Fully Remote
Employees working from home or a designated remote location for the entire workweek, with no requirement for in-office presence.

### 3.2 Hybrid
Employees splitting their time between office and remote work (e.g., 3 days in office, 2 days remote).

### 3.3 Flexible Hours
Employees working remotely on certain days while maintaining standard core hours (10:00 AM – 3:00 PM local time) for collaboration.

---

## 4. Remote Work Requirements

### 4.1 Communication Expectations
- Maintain regular communication with team members and managers
- Respond to emails and messages within 2 hours during business hours
- Participate in scheduled meetings (both virtual and in-person)
- Use company-approved communication tools (Slack, Teams, Zoom)

### 4.2 Productivity Standards
- Complete work according to established deadlines
- Maintain professional appearance during video calls (camera on, appropriate background)
- Document work progress and challenges for team transparency
- Ensure availability during core working hours

### 4.3 Equipment and Security
- Provide a secure, distraction-free workspace
- Use company-approved devices or follow device policies for personal equipment use
- Maintain data security and confidentiality standards
- Report any IT issues to the help desk promptly

---

## 5. Approval Process

### 5.1 Initial Request
Employees must submit a remote work request through the HRIS platform (Workday) with:
- Proposed work arrangement (full, hybrid, flexible)
- Duration of arrangement (minimum 3 months)
- Justification for remote work necessity
- Expected impact on team collaboration

### 5.2 Manager Review
Managers must evaluate requests based on:
- Job function and operational requirements
- Employee performance and track record
- Team needs and coverage requirements
- Impact on collaboration and productivity

### 5.3 Approval Timeline
Requests should be reviewed within 10 business days of submission. Managers may approve, deny, or request modifications.

---

## 6. Remote Work Hours and Schedule

### 6.1 Core Hours
All remote employees must be available during core hours (10:00 AM – 3:00 PM local time) for collaboration and meetings.

### 6.2 Flexible Scheduling
Employees may adjust their schedules within reason as long as:
- Core hours are maintained
- Team collaboration needs are met
- Client or stakeholder requirements are satisfied

---

## 7. Data Security and Compliance

### 7.1 Information Protection
Remote employees must:
- Use company-approved security tools (VPN, encryption)
- Store sensitive data on secure company servers
- Follow all data handling protocols when working remotely
- Report security incidents immediately

### 7.2 Work Environment
Employees must maintain a professional work environment that:
- Protects company information
- Prevents unauthorized access to workspace
- Ensures appropriate privacy for confidential discussions

---

## 8. Benefits and Compensation

### 8.1 Work-Life Balance Support
Remote employees have access to the same benefits as in-office employees, including:
- Flexible scheduling
- Mental health resources
- Wellness stipends
- Professional development opportunities

### 8.2 Equipment Allowance
The company provides:
- Laptop or desktop computer (as needed)
- Internet connectivity allowance ($50/month for eligible employees)
- Software licenses required for job function
- Access to company resources and tools

---

## 9. Performance Management

### 9.1 Regular Check-ins
Remote employees must have regular performance check-ins with their manager:
- Monthly one-on-one meetings
- Quarterly goal reviews
- Annual performance evaluations

### 9.2 Documentation
Managers must document:
- Performance outcomes during remote work periods
- Challenges and improvements noted
- Feedback provided to remote employees

---

## 10. Policy Acknowledgment

All employees must acknowledge receipt and understanding of this policy annually through the company's learning management system. New hires must sign the acknowledgment during their first week of employment.
