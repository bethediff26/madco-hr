# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Revised:** September 1, 2026  
**Version:** 4.2  
**Approved By:** VP of Human Resources  
**Department:** Human Resources — Compensation & Benefits

## 1. Purpose

The Mad Company Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy establishes the framework for employee paid time off, including accrual rates, usage procedures, carryover provisions, and termination payouts. This policy is designed to promote work-life balance, support employee well-being, and maintain operational continuity across the organization.

All full-time and part-time employees are subject to the terms outlined herein. Contractors, temporary workers, and interns are not eligible for PTO accruals under this policy unless explicitly stated in a written agreement with the company.

---

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Full-Time Employees

Full-time employees (those regularly scheduled to work 30 or more hours per week) become eligible for PTO accrual beginning on their first day of employment. Accrual rates are determined by the employee's tenure tier, calculated based on continuous length of service with Mad Company.

### 2.2 Part-Time Employees

Part-time employees (those regularly scheduled to work fewer than 30 hours per week) accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis relative to their scheduled hours compared to a full-time schedule. For example, an employee scheduled at 50% of full-time capacity will accrue PTO at 50% of the applicable full-time rate. Part-time employees must complete a minimum of 90 calendar days of employment before first using accrued PTO.

### 2.3 Probationary Employees

Employees within their initial probationary period (first 90 days of employment) may accrue PTO but are not permitted to use any accrued balance until the conclusion of the probationary period, except in cases of emergency as determined at the discretion of HR.

---

## 3. Annual Accrual Rates by Tenure Tier

PTO accrual is organized into three tenure tiers:

| Tenure Tier | Length of Service | Annual PTO Allowance | Monthly Accrual (Salaried, 40-hr/wk) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Less than 1 year | 15 days (120 hours) | 10.0 hours |
| Tier 2 | 1 to 3 years | 20 days (160 hours) | 13.3 hours |
| Tier 3 | 4+ years | 25 days (200 hours) | 16.7 hours |

### 3.1 Tenure Calculation

Length of service is calculated from the employee's original hire date with Mad Company. Re-hires who previously separated and were re-employed shall have their prior tenure credited if separation was in good standing and at least six months elapsed between the last day and rehire date. Continuous service resets only upon a break in employment exceeding 180 calendar days.

### 3.2 Accrual Mechanics

PTO accrues semi-monthly (on the 15th and final day of each month) for salaried employees and bi-weekly for hourly employees. Accrual stops during any unpaid leave period, including unpaid portions of FMLA leave and unpaid administrative leave.

### 3.3 Tenure Tier Transitions

When an employee crosses into a higher tenure tier, the increased accrual rate takes effect on the first payroll cycle following the anniversary date that triggers the transition. The employee's existing accrued PTO balance is not adjusted; only future accruals at the new rate are applied.

## 4. PTO Request Procedures

### 4.1 Advance Notice Requirements

Employees must submit PTO requests through the company's HRIS platform (Workday) a minimum of **two (2) weeks** in advance for any single day of absence and a minimum of **four (4) weeks** in advance for any consecutive stretch of three or more days. All submitted requests shall be subject to manager approval based on operational need and team coverage requirements.

### 4.2 Emergency PTO

In cases of documented emergency (including but not limited to personal illness, family medical crisis, or domestic emergency), the two-week advance notice requirement may be waived. Employees must notify their direct manager by phone or email as soon as practicable before their intended absence and submit a retroactive PTO request in Workday within three (3) business days of the onset of the emergency. Approval of emergency PTO requests remains at the discretion of the manager and HR department, supported by appropriate documentation where required.

### 4.3 Request Submission Window

PTO requests must be submitted through Workday with the following minimum information:
- Desired date(s) of absence
- Total hours or days requested
- Brief reason category (vacation, personal, medical, etc.)
- Confirmation that team coverage has been discussed

Managers are expected to respond to PTO requests within three (3) business days of submission. Requests denied due to operational constraints shall be accompanied by a written explanation and an offer of alternative dates when feasible.

---

## 5. Manager Approval Workflow and Escalation Path

### 5.1 Standard Approval Process

1. **Employee Submission:** Employee submits PTO request in Workday.
2. **Manager Review:** Direct manager reviews for coverage impact within 3 business days.
3. **Approval or Denial:** Manager approves, denies, or requests modification of the requested dates.
4. **Notification:** Employee receives automated notification and calendar block upon approval.

### 5.2 Escalation Path

If a PTO request is denied by a direct manager, the employee may escalate through the following chain:

1. **First-Level Escalation:** Director or department head (for individual contributors) / VP (for managers).
2. **Second-Level Escalation:** HR Business Partner assigned to the employee's department.
3. **Final Appeal:** HR Director may render a binding determination in consultation with the employee's department VP if the escalation involves scheduling conflicts that materially affect both operational needs and employee rights under this policy.

Escalations must be filed within five (5) business days of the initial denial and must include written justification for why the PTO should be granted despite the stated operational concerns.

---

## 6. Carryover Limits and Use-It-or-Lose-It Provisions

### 6.1 Standard Carryover

Employees may carry over a maximum of **five (5) days (40 hours)** of accrued but unused PTO into the subsequent calendar year. Any accruals beyond this five-day ceiling will be forfeited on December 31 of the current calendar year. No payout shall be made for forfeited amounts.

### 6.2 VP Approval Exception

Employees who have accrued more than five days of PTO may submit a written request to the Vice President of Human Resources for an exception allowing carryover of up to ten (10) additional days (for a maximum total carryover of fifteen days). Such requests must be filed no later than November 30 of each year and are subject to VP review based on:
- The employee's accrual balance as of October 31.
- Demonstrated attempts by the employee to schedule and take available PTO throughout the calendar year.
- Operational impact analysis provided by the employee's department head.

VP-granted exceptions must be documented in Workday with the VP's digital authorization attached.

### 6.3 Use-It-or-Lose-It Encouragement

HR will issue quarterly reminders to all employees regarding their current PTO balances and recommend minimum monthly usage targets based on tenure tier. Employees who consistently fail to take reasonable amounts of PTO over two consecutive quarters may be counseled by their manager and HR about the importance of taking earned time off.

---

## 7. Blackout Periods

### 7.1 Designated Scheduling Blackout Windows

The following blackout periods are established annually, during which PTO requests for more than two consecutive days will not be approved except in documented emergency situations:

- **Q4 Earnings Window:** December 26 through January 5 (inclusive) — aligns with fiscal year-end close.
- **Quarterly Business Review Weeks:** The week of the quarterly strategic planning session dates, as communicated annually by the Office of the CEO.
- **End-of-Month Close Period:** The last three business days of each month for finance, accounting, and reporting-related departments as designated by the CFO.

### 7.2 Requests During Blackout Periods

PTO requests of one (1) day or fewer during blackout periods remain subject to standard approval discretion. Managers are encouraged to grant such requests when operationally feasible but may deny without escalation obligation.

---

## 8. Termination and Payout Rules

### 8.1 Voluntary Resignation

Upon voluntary resignation, employees who provide a minimum of two (2) weeks' written notice shall be paid out for all accrued but unused PTO at their final regular rate of pay in accordance with applicable state law. Failure to provide the required notice period may result in forfeiture of PTO payout at the company's discretion, subject to state legal requirements.

### 8.2 Involuntary Termination

Employees terminated for any reason (including layoff, performance-related separation, or termination for cause) shall be paid out all accrued but unused PTO at their final regular rate of pay within the timeframe required by applicable state wage-and-hour law. This payout will appear on the employee's final paycheck or within the statutory deadline if later.

### 8.3 Retirement or Death

In the event of employee retirement or death, accrued PTO shall be paid out in full to the employee (or their estate) at the standard payout rate, and this payment shall be processed within ten (10) business days of the separation date.

### 8.4 State Law Supremacy

Where applicable state or local law mandates a higher accrual utilization guarantee or mandatory PTO payout upon separation, this policy's provisions shall defer to such legal requirements. The HR department reviews all state and local employment regulations semi-annually to ensure compliance.

---

## 9. HR Appeal Process for PTO Disputes

Employees who believe their PTO request was improperly denied or their accrual balance was miscalculated may file a formal appeal through the following process:

1. **Informal Resolution (Days 1–5):** Employee discusses the concern directly with their HR Business Partner, who will attempt to resolve the matter informally within five (5) business days.
2. **Formal Written Appeal (Days 6–10):** If informal resolution is unsuccessful, the employee submits a written appeal via Workday's HR Case Management module to the HR Director, including supporting documentation and a statement of the desired remedy.
3. **HR Director Determination (Days 11–20):** The HR Director reviews the appeal and issues a written determination within ten (10) business days of receipt. This determination is final and binding on both the employee and the organization.

---

## 10. Policy Acknowledgment

All employees are required to acknowledge receipt and understanding of this policy annually through the company's learning management system. Failure to complete annual acknowledgment may result in suspension of PTO requesting privileges until compliance is achieved.
