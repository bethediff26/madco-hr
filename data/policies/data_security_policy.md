# Data Security and Confidentiality Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Revised:** September 1, 2026  
**Version:** 4.3  
**Approved By:** General Counsel / VP of Human Resources  
**Department:** Human Resources — Compliance & Ethics  

---

## 1. Purpose

The Mad Company Data Security and Confidentiality Policy establishes the framework for protecting company information, employee data, client records, and proprietary assets. This policy ensures compliance with applicable laws and regulations while maintaining the trust of our stakeholders and partners.

All employees, contractors, and representatives are required to comply with this policy in all aspects of their work activities.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All company information systems, networks, and data
- All employee-created, received, or accessed information
- All third-party data and resources used in the course of business
- All personal devices used for company purposes (BYOD)
- All physical and digital storage locations

---

## 3. Information Classification

### 3.1 Confidential Information
Information that is:
- Not publicly available
- Has commercial value to the company
- Is protected by confidentiality agreements or legal restrictions
- Includes but is not limited to: financial data, strategic plans, client information, trade secrets, and employee records

### 3.2 Public Information
Information that is:
- Available to the public
- Not subject to confidentiality restrictions
- Can be freely shared without risk to company interests

### 3.3 Restricted Information
Information with special handling requirements:
- Highly sensitive data requiring additional protection measures
- Protected by specific legal requirements or industry standards
- Subject to strict access controls and audit requirements

---

## 4. Data Handling Requirements

### 4.1 Access Control
Employees must:
- Use only company-approved accounts and systems
- Follow the principle of least privilege (access only what's necessary)
- Report unauthorized access attempts immediately
- Log out of systems when leaving workstations

### 4.2 Data Protection
Employees must:
- Encrypt sensitive data in transit and at rest
- Use strong passwords and multi-factor authentication
- Regularly update software and security patches
- Avoid using public or unsecured networks for company business

### 4.3 Data Storage
All company data must be:
- Stored on approved systems
- Backed up regularly according to retention policies
- Protected by appropriate access controls
- Retained only as long as required by law or business need

---

## 5. Information Sharing and Transmission

### 5.1 Internal Sharing
- Share information only with authorized individuals
- Use company-approved communication channels
- Include appropriate confidentiality notices in shared documents
- Document all information transfers

### 5.2 External Sharing
- Obtain proper authorization before sharing sensitive data externally
- Use secure file transfer methods for large or sensitive files
- Implement non-disclosure agreements when required
- Follow legal requirements for data sharing and protection

---

## 6. Incident Response

### 6.1 Reporting Requirements
Employees must report any actual or suspected security incidents:
- Within 24 hours of discovery
- To their direct manager and the IT Security team
- Through the company's incident reporting system (Workday)

### 6.2 Response Procedures
The company will:
- Investigate all reported incidents promptly
- Implement appropriate containment measures
- Notify affected parties when required by law
- Document all actions taken during incident resolution

---

## 7. Personal Device Usage

### 7.1 Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)
Employees using personal devices for work must:
- Install and maintain company-approved security software
- Use strong passwords and biometric authentication
- Enable remote wipe capabilities if required
- Report lost or stolen devices immediately

### 7.2 Device Security
Personal devices must meet minimum security requirements:
- Up-to-date operating system and security patches
- Company-approved encryption tools
- Regular security scanning and updates
- Secure network access practices

---

## 8. Training and Awareness

### 8.1 Mandatory Training
All employees must complete annual data security training through the company's learning management system, covering:
- Password security best practices
- Phishing recognition and prevention
- Data classification and handling procedures
- Incident reporting processes

### 8.2 Ongoing Education
Employees are encouraged to participate in:
- Security awareness workshops
- Advanced training for technical roles
- Industry updates on security threats and best practices

---

## 9. Compliance and Enforcement

### 9.1 Progressive Discipline Framework
Violations of this policy will be addressed through the following framework:

| Level | Action | Applicable For |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verbal counseling and documentation | First-time minor violations |
| 2 | Written warning in personnel file | Repeat minor violations or first moderate violation |
| 3 | Final written warning with performance improvement plan | Continued violations after Level 2 |
| 4 | Termination of employment | Severe violations, repeated Level 3 infractions, or gross misconduct |

### 9.2 Legal Compliance
The company ensures compliance with:
- Federal and state privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
- Industry-specific regulations (HIPAA, SOX, etc.)
- Contractual obligations with clients and partners
- Internal audit and compliance requirements

---

## 10. Policy Acknowledgment

All employees must acknowledge receipt and understanding of this policy annually through the company's learning management system. New hires must sign the acknowledgment during their first week of employment.
