# Company Holidays Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Revised:** September 1, 2026  
**Version:** 2.8  
**Approved By:** VP of Human Resources  
**Department:** Human Resources — Compensation & Benefits  

---

## 1. Purpose

The Mad Company Holidays Policy establishes the framework for company-wide holidays and observances to ensure consistent treatment of all employees while respecting diverse cultural and religious needs. This policy supports employee well-being, work-life balance, and organizational unity.

All full-time and part-time employees are eligible for company holidays subject to their employment status and tenure requirements.

---

## 2. Company Holidays

### 2.1 Standard Company Holidays (Paid)
The following company holidays are observed annually with paid time off:
- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Third Monday in January)
- Presidents' Day (Third Monday in February)
- Memorial Day (Last Monday in May)
- Independence Day (July 4)
- Labor Day (First Monday in September)
- Columbus Day (Second Monday in October)
- Veterans Day (November 11)
- Thanksgiving Day (Fourth Thursday in November)
- Christmas Day (December 25)

### 2.2 Additional Observances
The company recognizes the following additional observances:
- Kwanzaa (December 26 - January 1, 2027)
- Hanukkah (December 24 - December 30, 2026)
- Diwali (November 12 - November 18, 2026)

---

## 3. Holiday Scheduling

### 3.1 Holiday Coverage
When a company holiday falls on a weekend:
- The preceding Friday or following Monday will be observed as a paid holiday
- For holidays that fall on non-working days, employees will receive compensatory time off in the following work week

### 3.2 Personal Holidays
Employees may request personal holidays for:
- Religious observances not covered by standard company holidays
- Cultural celebrations important to the employee
- Family emergencies or special circumstances

Personal holiday requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance and are subject to manager approval.

---

## 4. Holiday Accrual and Usage

### 4.1 Accrual Requirements
Employees accrue paid time off for company holidays based on their PTO policy:
- Full-time employees: Standard PTO accrual rates apply
- Part-time employees: Pro-rated PTO accruals apply

### 4.2 Holiday Usage Limits
Employees may use company holidays in combination with other PTO as per the PTO policy, but must follow all applicable advance notice requirements.

---

## 5. Special Circumstances

### 5.1 Company Events
The company may observe special events such as:
- Company anniversaries
- Product launches or major achievements
- Industry conferences or events (when relevant to employee roles)

These events may be designated as paid time off based on business needs and employee availability.

### 5.2 Emergency Situations
In cases of emergency or natural disasters affecting the company's operations:
- Employees may be required to work or adjust their schedules
- Emergency situations will be communicated through official channels
- Compensation or time off adjustments will be made in accordance with applicable law

---

## 6. Religious Accommodations

### 6.1 Request Process
Employees seeking religious accommodation for holidays must:
- Submit a written request to HR at least two weeks prior to the requested holiday
- Provide documentation of the religious observance when required
- Demonstrate that the accommodation does not cause undue hardship to the company

### 6.2 Reasonable Accommodations
The company will make reasonable accommodations for employees' religious practices, including:
- Alternative scheduling arrangements
- Time off for religious observances
- Flexible work arrangements during religious holidays

---

## 7. Policy Acknowledgment

All employees must acknowledge receipt and understanding of this policy annually through the company's learning management system. New hires must sign the acknowledgment during their first week of employment.
