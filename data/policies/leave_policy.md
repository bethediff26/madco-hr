**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## 1. Purpose

The Mad Company Leave of Absence Policy establishes the framework for employees to take time off from work for personal, family, or medical reasons while maintaining their employment relationship with the company. This policy ensures that employees can address important life circumstances while protecting their job security and benefits.

All eligible employees may request leave of absence under the conditions outlined in this policy.

---

## 2. Types of Leave

### 2.1 Sick Leave
- **Purpose**: For personal illness, injury, or medical appointments
- **Accrual**: Paid sick leave accrues according to PTO policy
- **Maximum**: Up to 10 days per calendar year for full-time employees
- **Documentation**: May require medical documentation for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days

### 2.2 Family Medical Leave
- **Purpose**: For serious health conditions affecting employee or family members
- **Eligibility**: Employees with at least 12 months of service and 1,250 hours worked in the previous 12 months
- **Duration**: Up to 12 weeks (unpaid) under federal law, with company-paid time for up to 6 weeks
- **Documentation**: Medical certification required

### 2.3 Maternity/Paternity Leave
- **Purpose**: For pregnancy, childbirth, and bonding with newborn child
- **Duration**: 
  - Maternity: Up to 12 weeks (paid)
  - Paternity: Up to 4 weeks (paid)
  - Adoption: Up to 12 weeks (paid)
- **Documentation**: Medical or adoption documentation required

### 2.4 Bereavement Leave
- **Purpose**: For death of immediate family members
- **Duration**: 
  - Immediate family (spouse, child, parent): 3 days
  - Extended family: 1 day
- **Documentation**: No formal documentation required

### 2.5 Personal Leave
- **Purpose**: For personal reasons not covered by other leave types
- **Duration**: Up to 5 days per calendar year for full-time employees
- **Documentation**: No formal documentation required

---

## 3. Eligibility Requirements

### 3.1 General Eligibility
To be eligible for paid leave:
- Must be a full-time employee (30+ hours/week)
- Must have completed at least 6 months of continuous employment
- Must meet specific requirements for each leave type

### 3.2 Part-Time Employees
Part-time employees may request unpaid leave with manager approval. Paid leave eligibility is pro-rated based on hours worked.

---

## 4. Application Process

### 4.1 Advance Notice
- **Sick Leave**: As soon as possible, with documentation within 3 business days
- **Family Medical Leave**: At least 30 days in advance when possible
- **Maternity/Paternity/Adoption**: At least 60 days in advance for planned events
- **Bereavement Leave**: Immediate notification required

### 4.2 Required Information
Leave requests must include:
- Type of leave requested
- Expected duration
- Reason for leave
- Contact information during absence
- Any supporting documentation

### 4.3 Manager Review
Managers will review all requests within 5 business days and respond with approval, denial, or request for additional information.

---

## 5. Benefits During Leave

### 5.1 Health Insurance
- Paid leave: Full health insurance coverage maintained
- Unpaid leave: Coverage may be suspended based on duration

### 5.2 PTO Accrual
- Paid leave: PTO continues to accrue at normal rates
- Unpaid leave: PTO accrual stops during leave period

### 5.3 Retirement Contributions
- Paid leave: Full retirement contributions continue
- Unpaid leave: Contributions may be suspended based on duration

---

## 6. Return to Work Procedures

### 6.1 Notice Requirements
Employees must notify their manager of return date at least 2 weeks in advance for planned returns.

### 6.2 Return Documentation
- Medical clearance required for extended medical leaves
- Re-integration plan may be developed for longer absences
- Support provided for successful reintegration

### 6.3 Job Protection
Employees returning from approved leave are entitled to:
- Re-entry into their original position or equivalent
- Protection from discrimination
- Restoration of benefits and privileges

---

## 7. Special Circumstances

### 7.1 Military Leave
Employees called for military duty may be eligible for:
- Unpaid leave up to 12 months
- Job protection under federal law
- Re-entry rights upon return

### 7.2 Educational Leave
Employees pursuing education may request:
- Up to 6 months of unpaid educational leave
- Flexible scheduling arrangements
- Tuition assistance support

---

## 8. Policy Acknowledgment

All employees must acknowledge receipt and understanding of this policy annually through the company's learning management system. New hires must sign the acknowledgment during their first week of employment.
